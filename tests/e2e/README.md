# E2E scenarios

Each directory is one numbered scenario: a spec, committed baseline screenshots, and a generated `README.md` walkthrough. See [`docs/e2e-test-guidelines.md`](../../docs/e2e-test-guidelines.md).

| # | Scenario | Proves |
|---|---|---|
| 001 | [App shell and seats](001-app-shell-and-seats/README.md) | The table loads with six seats; a player takes the bottom seat; it survives a reload |
| 002 | [Phone takes a seat](002-phone-takes-a-seat/README.md) | A phone opens a seat's QR link, takes the seat through the relay, and sees its private view; the table shows only the public rail |
| 003 | [Africa board and six seats](003-africa-board-and-six-seats/README.md) | The hex map of Africa with its thirteen ports fills the table between six seat rails; taking a seat leaves the board unchanged; it survives a reload |
| 004 | [Known Africa](004-known-africa/README.md) | The pre-printed periphery of 1820 is drawn: terrain symbols, the twelve printed rivers with drainage numbers, and the two printed cataracts; the interior stays blank |
| 005 | [Commission an explorer](005-commission-an-explorer/README.md) | A taken seat offers the four specialties and a confirm; the explorer token appears on the rail in the seat colour with a badge per specialty; a second seat commissions an ordinary explorer |
| 006 | [First player and donations](006-first-player-and-donations/README.md) | Starting the game rolls four dice per explorer in view, marks the high roller, deals five face-up donation cards to each rail and puts the piles on the board; all of it replays from the seed |
| 007 | [A turn in Europe](007-a-turn-in-europe/README.md) | A Europe turn draws a card; end-turn needs confirmation at the edge and passes the marker clockwise; unused free tickets expire at the owner's next turn; a hand over five must be trimmed by tapping cards before drawing |
| 008 | [Sail to Africa](008-sail-to-africa/README.md) | The sail control turns the ports into a chooser showing ticket, fare or unaffordable; booking makes the hand the budget, the explorer skips a turn at sea and lands on the port hex |
| 009 | [Outfit the expedition](009-outfit-the-expedition/README.md) | At the port a panel at the player's edge sells the price list with ×1/×10 steps, port restrictions, a running budget and a carrying summary; confirming forfeits the rest and the rail lists the expedition |
| 010 | [First step into the unknown](010-first-step-into-the-unknown/README.md) | From the port the expedition targets one adjacent hex; a blank one rolls the lost check at the rail; lost means staying put; success generates and draws the terrain, places the marker and moves the token |
| 011 | [Rivers, oases and cataracts](011-rivers-and-oases/README.md) | Mapped hexes gain river sources, continuations through rolled exits, cataracts with waterfall heights and mountain peak heights, all drawn on the board and replayable |
| 012 | [Movement rates](012-movement-rates/README.md) | The first step of a turn fixes the mode and a budget of movement points shown as pips at the rail: four mounted with unknown hexes costing two, four by canoe along water only; a hex that proves impassable for the mode is mapped and claimed but turns the expedition back |
| 013 | [Disaster cards](013-disaster-cards/README.md) | Each turn in the field outside a port or Cape Colony draws a card at the rail: a card matching the terrain takes its toll from the chart with a note, one for another terrain shows no effect, and an animal attack is shot at with a die per hunter |
| 014 | [Hunting and rations](014-hunting-and-rations/README.md) | Ending a turn in the field rolls the hunting die with the camp's modifiers; the hunters' bag feeds the men first, then the stores; the die and the meal stay at the explorer's rail while the others play and the food count on the chart drops |
| 015 | [Water in the desert](015-water-in-the-desert/README.md) | Water is taken or poured ten at a time at the rail wherever there is water; a night in the dry desert costs one per man and four per animal; short of water the bearers die of thirst and the camels are shot for meat |
| 016 | [Natives discovered and the explorer's policy](016-natives-discovered-and-policy/README.md) | Entering a new hex rolls two dice for natives; found, three dice make the warriors, a numbered tribe marker goes on the hex and a panel at the player's edge asks for a policy; retreat returns to the previous hex and lifts the marker; a known tribe asks again without a roll |
| 017 | [Native reaction and combat](017-native-reaction-and-combat/README.md) | After the policy the natives react on three dice against distance plus policy; hostile, a strength die decides hide or attack; an attack rolls surprise, the volley, and alternating strikes until one side is finished, every die and the story of the fight at the rail; a weakened tribe hides |
| 018 | [Defeat, escape and victory](018-defeat-escape-and-victory/README.md) | A victory takes prisoners and finds the village, and a panel at the edge chooses between releasing them and keeping them to loot a die per warrior; a later defeat rolls the explorer's fate: held prisoner with the expedition lost, then an escape roll each turn until free, alone |
| 019 | [Negotiation and trade](019-negotiation-and-trade/README.md) | A calm chief receives gifts on a panel at the edge: gifts times the larger die must reach the warriors; satisfied, he rolls a price per item and the trade panel hires a bearer and sells rations by the gift; giving everything but the men returns three dice of gifts |
| 020 | [Tribe attitude](020-tribe-attitude/README.md) | Releasing every prisoner earns the tribe's regard for that explorer; the policy panel shows the tribe's attitude and the regard, and the next reaction counts them |
| 021 | [Sickness, starvation and desertion](021-sickness-starvation-desertion/README.md) | Out of rations the hungry desert or fall sick by the die, guarded bearers stay; sick men who cannot be carried halt the march with a mark at the rail; each evening the sick recover or die by the die, worse when starving |
| 022 | [Specialties in the field](022-specialties-in-the-field/README.md) | A missionary preaches once the chief permits, moving the tribe's attitude and banking points before trading can end; a zoologist owes each new hex a turn of study instead of moving, and a die by the terrain finds a specimen shown on the chart |
| 023 | [Caches and reorganisation](023-caches-and-reorganisation/README.md) | A panel at the edge caches rations and muskets under a marker on the hex and dismisses bearers; next turn the cache is dug up with a die and its goods rejoin the chart |
| 024 | [Return to Europe](024-return-to-europe/README.md) | At a port of entry the rail offers the voyage home: the expedition is dismissed, the token goes to sea, and the next turn opens in Europe with a card drawn |
| 025 | [Publishing and points](025-publishing-and-points/README.md) | Two explorers claim the same hex; the first home opens the claim on the board and publishes it: the hex is inked, both markers come off, two points tally at the rail, a card is drawn, and the other explorer lands with nothing to publish |
| 026 | [Death and retirement](026-death-and-retirement/README.md) | A defeat die of 1 kills the explorer: his hex is erased with its tribe, the rail shows the obituary and the turn ends as usual; the seat commissions a zoologist who draws on his first turn; another player retires his explorer from Europe by tapping him and confirming at the edge |
| 027 | [Superlatives](027-superlatives/README.md) | The first step from Mombasa maps a mountain with a river and a 22,000 ft peak shown in brown; published from Europe it scores the river, nothing for the mountain, and eight for the highest peak known, nine points tallied at the rail |
| 028 | [Epic transits](028-epic-transits/README.md) | A trek from Mombasa to Khartoum (replayed from a recorded event log) ends with a step onto Khartoum: the rail announces the first crossing to the east coast; home in Europe every route hex is published for its terrain plus a bonus point |
| 029 | [Joint expeditions](029-joint-expeditions/README.md) | Sailing from Europe, bottom-1 pools top-1's donations; they land together, the leader outfits one chart, the party steps into a hex as one on top-1's turn with both tokens side by side, and top-1 leaves with three bearers and ten rations chosen at the edge |
| 030 | [Game end and continuation](030-game-end-and-continuation/README.md) | A five-round limit is set beside the start control; when round five ends the explorer home in Europe publishes his hex, everyone retires, the standings name the winner in the middle of the table, and a tap continues on the same map with the published hex still inked and the seats commissioning anew |
| 031 | [Reload and undo](031-reload-and-undo/README.md) | Booking a voyage and outfitting are taken back with the undo control at the rail; a step that rolled the dice cannot be; a reload in the middle of a turn, and another during a discard, bring back the same screen |
| 032 | [Responsive pass at 27" and 55"](032-responsive-27-and-55/README.md) | Six seats fill the table at 1920×1080 as a 27" table and (in the `table-4k` project) at 3840×2160 as a 55" one: the touch target, cards and rails are sized from the configured diagonal, the port chooser and the outfit panel fit between six rails, the hex pitch is measured, and the same game reconfigured for a screen of half the diagonal grows its controls to 78 px and still fits |
